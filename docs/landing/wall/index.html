The wall, on a Tab5

The start page's wall on its own screen: the clock, the weather and the hours ahead, today's and the week's events, birthdays, tasks to tick off, your Home Assistant tiles and your ioPix photos. Add a task or an event from it, set a timer, wake up to its alarm clock.

  1. Connect the Tab5 to this computer with a USB-C cable that carries data, and open this page in Chrome or Edge on a computer (they can write to a USB device; Safari and Firefox cannot).
  2. Put the Tab5 in download mode: hold its reset button for about two seconds, until the green light inside blinks fast, then let go.
  3. Press Install, pick the Tab5's port (often “USB JTAG/serial debug unit”) and wait about a minute. When it says it is done, press the reset button once.
  4. On the Tab5, pick your Wi-Fi, type your start page's address, and let it in: scan its code with your phone (or open your start page at #/pair and type the code). It can also sign in with your name and password.
This browser cannot write to a USB device. Open this page in Chrome or Edge on a computer. USB needs a secure page: open https://iosuite.iothing.net/wall/. Download the firmware

Installing erases the Tab5 (its own demo, and anything else on it). Once it is signed in, it updates itself at night; Settings on the device turns that off. To put M5Stack's own firmware back, use M5Burner.
