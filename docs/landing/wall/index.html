esp32-p4 touch screens

The wall, on its own screen

The start page's wall on a small touch screen: the clock, the weather and the hours ahead, today's and the week's events, birthdays, tasks to tick off, your Home Assistant tiles and your ioPix photos. Add a task or an event from it, set a timer, wake up to its alarm clock.

For an M5Stack Tab5 (5 inch) or a Waveshare ESP32-P4-WIFI6-Touch-LCD-7B (7 inch). Open this page in Chrome or Edge on a computer: they can write to a USB device, Safari and Firefox cannot.

M5Stack Tab5

  1. Connect the Tab5 to this computer with a USB-C cable that carries data.
  2. Put the Tab5 in download mode: hold its reset button for about two seconds, until the green light inside blinks fast, then let go.
  3. Press Install, pick the Tab5's port (often “USB JTAG/serial debug unit”) and wait about a minute. When it says it is done, press the reset button once.
  4. On the Tab5, pick your Wi-Fi, type your start page's address, and let it in: scan its code with your phone (or open your start page at #/pair and type the code). It can also sign in with your name and password.
This browser cannot write to a USB device. Open this page in Chrome or Edge on a computer. USB needs a secure page: open https://iosuite.iothing.net/wall/. Download the firmware

Installing erases the Tab5 (its own demo, and anything else on it). To put M5Stack's own firmware back, use M5Burner.

Waveshare ESP32-P4-WIFI6-Touch-LCD-7B

  1. Connect the board to this computer with a USB-C cable that carries data. Of its two USB-C ports, the one for USB to UART is the easier.
  2. Press the Install that fits your board (below), pick its port (a “USB Serial” port) and wait about a minute. If it cannot connect, hold the board's BOOT button, press RESET once, let go of BOOT, and try again.
  3. When it says it is done, press RESET once.
  4. On the screen, pick your Wi-Fi, type your start page's address, and let it in, as on the Tab5.

The board comes with one of two generations of the ESP32-P4 chip, and each needs its own firmware: newer boards have revision 3, earlier ones revision 1. Nothing breaks when you pick the wrong one: the screen stays dark, and you install the other. Installing erases the board's own demo; Waveshare's page for the board has that firmware, to put it back.

Once a screen is signed in, it updates itself at night; Settings on the device turns that off.
